Language model comparison

Which Ollama models work best in Assist Router with Polish: accuracy and time in each role. Test of 21.10.2026 on an NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin 64 GB.

← Assist Router documentation · Polski · English · GitHub

The test was run only with Polish questions and Polish data (the bundled language pack is Polish), so these results apply to Polish. In another language the results may look different; you can repeat the test for your language (see below).

What was tested

Every model went through the same parts of the test, in the roles it plays in Assist Router:

Test partWhat it checksNumber of cases
Router (topic)whether the model picks the right topic from router.yaml for a free-form question, also with speech recognition errors40 (round 1), 96, then 101
Answers: given dataan answer from ready-made data: counting hours and amounts, “in how many minutes”, “the day after tomorrow”, no data in the calendar, a large context19
Answers: HA dataan answer from a real snapshot of the home's data: school timetable, calendars, chores, car, sensors34
Wikipedia summariesan article summary in 3–4 sentences, with the key fact and without exceeding the length8

An answer counts as correct when it contains the expected facts (e.g. a time or an amount) and does not contain forbidden ones (e.g. an event from another day). Time is the average time of one answer; models were loaded and unloaded one after another, so they did not compete for memory.

Colors: ≥ 95%≥ 85%below 85% The note “round 1/2” marks a result from a smaller set in an earlier round.

Conclusions

  • Answer model: Gemma 4 26B (MoE, about 4B parameters active at a time): 33/34 on HA data in 1.2 s and 8/8 summaries in 5.5 s. The best ratio of quality to time.
  • Router: Gemma 4 E4B recognizes 97/101 topics in 0.55 s. Ministral 3 8B was just as accurate, but slower and weaker at answers; Gemma 4 E2B is the fastest (0.39 s) at 90/96.
  • One model in both roles: when memory allows, Gemma 4 26B as both router and answer model keeps only one model in memory. This is how the author runs it today (section 3 of the documentation: 153/153 topics, about 0.7 s).
  • Lightweight setup: Gemma 4 E4B in both roles – 8/8 summaries in 3.8 s, but worse at calculating with HA data (29/34: sums, counting days).
  • Polish models: Bielik Minitron 7B answered best from HA data (34/34), but more slowly (2.7 s) and its summaries were too long (18 s). Bielik 11B Q6 has similar accuracy on data (33/34), but is about 4.5× slower than Gemma 4 26B. PLLuM (4B/8B/12B) recognizes topics well, but answers from data less well.
  • Small models (Qwen 3.5 0.8B–4B, PLLuM 4B) are good enough at most as a router; in answers they mix up numbers and days.

Typical model errors

  • Arithmetic: sums of amounts, number of hours („5 i pół godziny”, “5 and a half hours”), “in how many minutes” until the next meeting – the most common slips, even for large models.
  • Days of the week: „pojutrze” (“the day after tomorrow”), „w przyszłym tygodniu” (“next week”) – events from the wrong day, or left out.
  • Confused topics: calendar instead of lessons or chores, “knowledge” instead of the tram timetable; questions with speech recognition errors ended up in “knowledge”.
  • Making things up: adding events that are not in the data, or “I don't know” although the data contained the answer.
  • Summaries: 6–8 sentences instead of 3–4 (Bielik, Ministral), or the key fact missing.
  • Form: markdown (**) and end-of-text markers in an answer that is meant to be read aloud.

Fine-tuning the router

  • First run: Gemma 4 E2B fine-tuned (LoRA) on 15 thousand sentences generated from hassil templates, 80 minutes on an RTX 4090. 298/300 on template sentences, but 88/96 on free-form questions – the model learned the templates instead of generalizing.
  • Second run: Gemma 4 E4B (QLoRA), 14 thousand examples, including 7.7 thousand paraphrases written by Gemma 4 26B. On 101 questions outside the training data 100/101 in 0.62 s (base Gemma 4 E4B: 97/101 in 0.55 s); validation 290/300 versus 272/300.
  • Conclusion: paraphrases written by a larger model are key. How to fine-tune the router on your own topics: section 13 of the documentation.

How to repeat the test yourself

The tools are in the repository's tools/ directory: snapshot_data.py (data snapshot), model_benchmark.py (model test) and model_report.py (a table like this one as HTML and CSV). Description: section 12 of the documentation.
